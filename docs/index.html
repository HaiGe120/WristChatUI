WristChatUI

Bring your own AI connection to iPhone and Apple Watch, with optional web search and source links alongside your conversations.

  • OpenAI Responses, OpenAI Chat Completions and Anthropic Messages formats for compatible endpoints.
  • Native search where supported, or an independently configured MiniMax plan search or remote HTTPS MCP search source.
  • Readable Watch replies, source details, and Stop/Retry recovery after interrupted requests.
  • Paired-device sync, optional iCloud conversation backups, Watch widgets and Siri Shortcuts.

You supply the endpoint, model and any required credentials. Search availability and usage limits depend on your configured services. ChatGPT subscription sign-in is not included.

Search for WristChatUI on the App Store. GitHub release downloads have their own signing and installation requirements; a downloaded IPA is not automatically installable.
